About Our Company

Manufacturing quality doors in the Philippines for over three decades

Our Story

Established in 1990, Filipinas Asia Shutter Door Corporation began with a simple mission: to provide durable, high-quality shutter doors for the growing industrial and commercial sectors in the Philippines.

Starting from a small workshop in Cainta, Rizal, we've grown into a trusted manufacturer supplying security and industrial doors to businesses nationwide.

Today, our 5,000 sqm manufacturing facility houses modern equipment operated by skilled craftsmen with decades of experience in metal fabrication and door systems.
